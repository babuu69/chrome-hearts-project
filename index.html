01 / THE HERITAGE

A Los Angeles Original

Chrome Hearts began in Los Angeles in 1988, growing from a small creative workshop into an independent luxury label known for its distinctive approach to jewelry, leather, clothing and objects.

Its visual language draws on motorcycle culture, rock music, handcraft and a fiercely individual point of view. The result is a world that feels recognisable without following the ordinary rules of fashion.
